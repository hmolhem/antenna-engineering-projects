\section{Evidence Availability}

The public package includes the screenshots used in the report, the LaTeX source,
analytical verification code, regenerated analytical figures, and machine-readable
result summaries. The native CST project, raw complex sweep data, mesh statistics,
solver log, port-mode field exports, and formal convergence records are not contained
in the supplied archive and are therefore not claimed as reproducibility assets.
