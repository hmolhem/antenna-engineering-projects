\section{Evidence Availability}

The public package includes all screenshots used in the report, the LaTeX source,
analytical verification code, and machine-readable key-result summaries. The native
CST project, raw curve exports, and solver/mesh logs are not included in the supplied
source archive and are therefore not claimed as reproducibility assets.
