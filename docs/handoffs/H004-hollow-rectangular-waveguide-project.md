## Verified Results

### Analytical baseline at 10 GHz

- TE10 and TE01 cutoff: 7.494811 GHz
- (1,1)-family cutoff: 10.599264 GHz
- Free-space wavelength: 29.979246 mm
- Lowest-order guide wavelength: 45.284113 mm
- Lowest-order TE wave impedance: 569.056941 ohm
- Electrical length: 1.987452 guide wavelengths

### CST port-eigenmode cutoffs

- Port 1: 7.490707, 7.491242, and 10.5935 GHz
- Port 2: 7.490310, 7.490640, and 10.5931 GHz
- Maximum relative difference from theory: 0.0601%

### Multimode behavior

- Same-mode input reflection remains below approximately -50 dB over the plotted 8-15 GHz band.
- Dominant transmission appears in the alternate degenerate output basis near 0 dB.
- These S-parameter magnitudes are visual estimates because raw exports are not included.
- The basis-index change is interpreted as degenerate-subspace rotation, not a verified physical discontinuity-driven mode conversion.

## Evidence Classification

- Analytical and simulated evidence only.
- No fabrication, VNA measurement, field-probe measurement, or horn-range validation is claimed.
- Native CST binaries are not included in ordinary Git history.
- Finite conductor loss and insertion loss are not claimed.

## Numerical Reliability

- No formal mesh-convergence record is included.
- No systematic port-polarization or eigenbasis sensitivity study was completed.
- The model uses vacuum filling and PEC walls.
- The square cross section makes the lowest-order modal basis non-unique.
- Only the first three solved port modes are documented.
- No horn flare, transition, or manufacturing-tolerance model is included.
